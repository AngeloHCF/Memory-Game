How to Use Memorize Anything

Step 1: Choose What to Memorize

Select an existing item from your list, or click "Create" to add something new (quotes, facts, anything you want to remember!).

Step 2: Start the Game

Click the "Start" button. The text will appear on screen for you to read and memorize.

Step 3: Begin Memorizing

When you're ready, press Enter to hide the text and start typing.

Step 4: Type What You Remember

Type the text from memory as accurately as you can. Press Enter to submit your guess.

Step 5: Review Your Answer

Correct letters appear in green, incorrect ones in red. Press Enter again to continue practicing.

Step 6: Check Your Progress

Click "History" to see all your attempts and track your improvement!

💡 Tips

  • Take your time reading - the text stays until you press Enter
  • Keep practicing until you can type it perfectly from memory
  • Use "Delete" to remove items you no longer need
  • Click "Stop" anytime to end your session
